# Trump is recruiting thousands of local officers to aid immigration effort. Some states are saying no #
By David A. Lieb and Brian Witte

Published by The Associated Press

[https://apnews.com/article/immigration-ice-trump-287g-72929a61953c3da5ed5d49cab2dcc611]([url](https://apnews.com/article/immigration-ice-trump-287g-72929a61953c3da5ed5d49cab2dcc611))

This story reports the number of agreements in the country that allow local law enforcement officers to interrogate and detain people suspected of being in the country illegally. It reports the total number agreements, breaks down that number by state.

The AP journalists use the article to explain how the agreements differ and the context of the agreements, like ICE giving local law enforcement agencies $100,000 for new vehicles. They frame that larger story around Maryland Governor Wes Moore signing a law Tuesday prohibiting such agreements. That leads to another data element of the story: the states that have outlawed the agreements.

The article is a good example of how to use data to create one event or press release - the signing of a law - into a national data story, how to accurately categorize items in a large dataset and how to report what's not in the dataset shared by the initial event.
