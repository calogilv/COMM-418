# All data journalism students have at least some published clips, according to survey  
## Blah blah blah

Everyone in Data-Driven Journalism has some journalism work published no matter what area of journalism they're most interested in.

The 17 class members, including Professor Theo Francis, all reported their journalism has been published to at least some degree. 

When asked the degree to which they've been published given a scale of zero to four, three people chose one, six people chose two, four people chose three and five people chose four. Nobody responded with a zero.

## Section header

Two who are interested in local news placed themselves at the far end of the publication frequency scale. One person interested in investigative reporting, one person interested in sports journalism and one person interested in business journalism (Francis, the professor) also rated themselves as a four.

But an interest in a particular area of journalism likely doesn't correlate to publication experience.
