# The Crash Stops #
## After Metro removed some bus stops for safety, drivers are still crashing near them. ##
By Caleb Ogilvie

[Methodology](https://github.com/calogilv/COMM-418/blob/main/The%20Crash%20Stops%20Methodology.md)

The Washington Metropolitan Area Transit Authority removed 527 bus stops on June 29, 2025, in part because of safety concerns. But in the eight months that followed, drivers crashed vehicles into pedestrians, buildings and other vehicles 3,890 times near the remaining bus stops in Washington, according to an analysis of crashes and bus stops.

Bus stops don’t necessarily cause crashes, but some stops are in areas where, for one reason or another, people crash vehicles more frequently. If people wait at those bus stops, they may see — or become part of — more crashes than they would otherwise. Some of the crashes have caused injuries and, in five cases, people have died because of a crash near a bus stop.

WMATA removed 527 bus stops in Washington, Maryland and Virginia on June 29, 2025, according to a May 2025 report of the removals. The authority assessed use and safety of the stops when deciding which to remove.

Between June 29, 2025 and Feb. 28, 8,553 people were in a crash near the bus stops that remain, according to the analysis of crashes occurring within 68 meters of WMATA bus stops. Five people died from a crash within the eight-month period and within 68 meters of a bus stop. In separate instances, 64 people sustained at least one major injury in a crash near a bus stop and 1,310 people sustained at least one minor injury.

3,890 crashes occured within 68 miles of a bus stop between June 29, 2025, and Feb. 28.

Major injuries can range from loss of teeth to severe lacerations, according to the documentation web page for the database on crashes. Minor injuries range from bruises to bleeding and swelling.

The North Capitol Street and N Street bus stop is on a right turn-only lane on North Capitol Street and near a large intersection conjoining six roads. As drivers approach the stop, they sandwich their cars between parallel parking spots on one side and guard rails on the other. 

Drivers have crashed near that bus stop 31 times between June 29, 2025, and Feb. 28, according to the analysis of crashes occurring within 68 meters of WMATA bus stops. It’s the bus stop where drivers crashed the most in the eight months.

The Malcolm X Avenue and S Capitol Street stop is at the beginning of an exit lane to Interstate 295. Drivers crashed near that bus stop 26 times between June 29, 2025, and Feb. 28. It’s the bus stop with the second-most crashes.

Other bus stops with the highest numbers of crashes are at intersections with at least four roads. Some are on the grid of streets in the center of the city. Others are in residential areas east of the Anacostia River.

Four of the bus stops are at the same intersection in downtown Washington: where U Street meets 14 Street. In total, 83 drivers have crashed within 68 meters of one of the bus stops at that intersection.

Several pedestrians have also been struck by vehicles near bus stops.

Marrion Barry Avenue ends at a five-way intersection. Drivers can turn left or right across six lanes of Alabama Avenue or they can head straight into a strip mall parking lot. A bus stop sits a few feet away from the parking lot entrance.
 
An eight-year-old and 29-year-old crashed at the intersection on July 20, 2025. The child received at least one minor injury.
