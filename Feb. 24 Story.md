# Is DC still ‘Chocolate City?’ Here’s what Census data tells us #
By Scott Gelman

Published by WTOP

[https://wtop.com/dc/2026/02/is-dc-still-chocolate-city-heres-what-census-data-tells-us/]([url](https://wtop.com/dc/2026/02/is-dc-still-chocolate-city-heres-what-census-data-tells-us/))

This story reports on Census data of Washington, D.C. between 2021 and 2024. It notes that, in 2024, 304,452 people identified as only Black or African American, meaning less than half of Washington residents are Black. It then uses expert interviews to explain how that happened.

I do not think this is a very good data story, however. More data could have been used to explain how less than half of Washingtonians are Black. How many people live in Washington, for example? How does that number compare to 2021, the initial point of comparison? The article says 305,972 residents identified as Black or African American in 2021. But in 2021, was that half of the population or more? Why use 2021 as a comparison point?
